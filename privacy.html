Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Vampire Town does not collect, upload or share any personal data.

  • The app does not use an internet connection and contains no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Player names and game settings you enter are stored only on your device and never leave it.
  • This data is removed when you delete the app. You can also delete players and reset settings inside the app.
  • The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
  • If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
